## 1x Backplate
reverse engineered plate to suit the gear bearing, the return spring and the blade holder

## 1x Blade holder
the blade holder is designed to hold the blade segment from generic cutter blade (9mm wide). Push it in place with hot soldering tip

## 1x Blade lock
the blade lock is needed to help the blade to maintain position, and give the place to put the return spring leg

## 1x Gear unlock lever
this is the lever to unlock the filament from the extruding gear, it is independent from the cutter lever, and can be operated by hand

## 1x Cutter lever
this is the cutter lever, I've tried to print it but PLA is too weak to do the work, so I decided to machine it from aluminum, but maybe harder material loaded with CF could do the trick
