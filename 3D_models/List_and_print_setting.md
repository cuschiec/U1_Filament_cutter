## 1x Backplate
reverse engineered plate to suit the gear bearing, the return spring and the blade holder.
  ### Print setting: 
    Layer: 0.2mm 
    Perimeters: 5
    Support: YES
    Support type: Normal
    Support Z top distance: 0.1mm
    Support Top interface spacing: 0.2mm
    Support Base pattern spacing: 1.0mm
  

## 1x Blade holder
the blade holder is designed to hold the blade segment from STANLEY 11-300 cutter blade (I think other blade can be compatible if less than 9.35mm wide, but model have to be modified), push the blade in place with hot soldering tip
  ### Print setting: 
    Layer: 0.2mm 
    Perimeters: 5
    Support: NO

## 1x Cutter lever PREFERABLE OPTION (aluminum milled)
